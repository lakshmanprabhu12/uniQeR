FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the price really one-time?

Yes. You pay once for your plan and there is no monthly fee for the page. We publish it on a free link. A custom domain is optional, and its yearly cost goes to the domain registrar.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera and your page opens in the browser.

Does the payment come through you?

No. Pay Now opens the customer's own UPI app with your UPI ID, so the money goes straight to you. We never touch it.

Is Pay Now really locked until they rate or follow?

It is a gentle nudge, not a hard lock. Customers are guided to rate, follow or join first, which helps you collect reviews and followers. Your UPI ID is still shown for anyone who prefers to pay directly.

Can I change timings, links or the UPI ID later?

Yes. Send the change on WhatsApp. Edits are free within the period in your plan, and later changes are quoted at a small fee.

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, business name, UPI ID, phone number, address, opening hours, and your Google review, Google Maps, Instagram and WhatsApp links. A licence number such as FSSAI is optional.

I run a shop or service, not a cafe. Will it fit?

Yes. We adapt the sections, wording and look to your business. Tell us what you do and we will shape it.
